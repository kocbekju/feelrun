Training, health and safety

  • Feelrun is for training support only. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Heart-rate limits and zones, the VO₂max estimate, plans and coaching are training aids. With a heart condition, an injury, during pregnancy or with any doubt about your health, ask your doctor before training to them.
  • If you feel pain, dizziness, chest pain, faintness or distress, stop and seek help.
  • Running, hiking and walking outdoors carry risk: falls, weather, terrain, darkness, traffic, getting lost. You decide where, when and how hard you go, and you are responsible for your own safety.
  • Alerts (heart-rate, distance and time limits, off-route, sunset) are aids. They depend on sensors, GPS, the battery and your settings, and may come late, be wrong or not come at all. Do not rely on them alone: take light, water and clothing for the outing, and tell someone where you are going.
  • Live sharing (FollowMe) is not an emergency service. It needs a connection and may stop. In an emergency, call the emergency number (112 in Europe).
  • Distance, pace, climb, heart rate and other measures come from Apple Watch, iPhone and Apple Health and may be inaccurate.

The AI coach

  • With your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key, Feelrun sends requests from your iPhone to that provider. Your use of the provider is under its own terms and policies, and it bills your account for what Feelrun asks. You are responsible for your key and its charges; you can set a spending limit with the provider and remove the key in Settings › AI at any time.
  • Answers, analyses, plans and cues from the AI coach are written by the provider's model. They can be wrong, incomplete or unsuited to you. Use your own judgement: they are not the advice of a coach, a doctor or a physiotherapist.
  • Feelrun asks before any training data is sent. The Privacy Policy says what is sent.
