Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Feelrun is a running and trail-running app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It has no user accounts and no server of its own.

What stays on your devices

  • Workouts, routes, heart rate and other data read from or written to Apple Health.
  • Workout history, analyses, coach chat, plans and your goal, stored in the app on your iPhone. Feelrun does not sync these through iCloud.
  • Your AI provider API key, stored in the iOS Keychain.

Feelrun's developer does not receive, collect, sell or share any of this data. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Purchases

Feelrun Pro and its free trial are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the app only asks the App Store whether Pro was bought or the trial started, and when. The developer receives no payment or personal details from it. In the trial, the app counts on your iPhone how many times live sharing and replay videos were used.

Optional AI features

If you turn on Remote AI, enter your own API key for a provider (for example OpenAI or Anthropic) and allow data sharing when asked, Feelrun sends the request directly from your iPhone to that provider under your account. Each request is built from: workout titles, dates and summaries (type, distance, duration, climb); heart rate, pace, elevation and terrain derived from your routes (kilometre splits and profile points, not coordinates); heart-rate zones and your heart-rate threshold; training load; your goal and plan; weather at your workouts and the forecast for the coming days; and your chat messages and notes. Raw GPS coordinates are not sent. The provider's own privacy policy governs that data. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings › AI & Privacy; without it, no data is sent and on-device coaching is used instead.

FollowMe (live sharing, optional)

If you start FollowMe, Feelrun writes your live location track, elapsed time, workout state and, on a known route, remaining distance to a temporary area in your own private iCloud database and creates a read-only link or QR code. Anyone you give the link to can follow your run in Feelrun while the workout lasts. Your heart rate and other Apple Health data are not part of it. Distance and pace shown to followers are calculated from the location track. The shared data is deleted when the workout ends (and leftovers are removed the next time you open the app); you can stop sharing at any time. Feelrun's developer cannot see it. Scanning a FollowMe code uses the camera; images are processed on the device and not stored.

Strava (optional)

If you connect Strava, Feelrun uploads the workouts it recorded (route, time, heart rate and elevation, as a GPX file) from your iPhone directly to Strava, under your own Strava account. Signing in, and renewing the sign-in, passes through a small server run by Feelrun's developer: it adds the application's secret and hands Strava's answer back. It stores and logs nothing, and workouts never pass through it. You can disconnect in Settings at any time; what was uploaded stays with Strava under Strava's terms.

Location

Location is used during workouts to record your route, show terrain ahead, warn when you leave a known route, and estimate sunset for daylight warnings. To name a run after the place it went to, Feelrun asks Apple's geocoder about the start and up to two more points of the run, each rounded to about 100 m. Location is not shared except as described here and above.

Motion & Fitness (barometer)

During a workout Feelrun reads the barometer (Motion & Fitness permission) to measure how much you climb. Only the resulting climb in metres is kept; pressure readings are not stored or shared. If you decline, climb is measured from GPS altitude instead.

Weather

To show conditions for a workout, Feelrun asks Apple WeatherKit for weather at the workout's location and time. For the training plan and the coach it also asks for the daily forecast near where you last ran. The location is used only to ask WeatherKit.

Photos

When you choose Save to Photos after creating a route replay video, Feelrun asks for add-only access and writes that video to your photo library. Feelrun does not read your photo library.

Children

Feelrun is not directed at children under 13.
